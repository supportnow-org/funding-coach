What FundingCoach is

A coach who has already funded what you are trying to fund

You are not handed a list of links and wished good luck. You are matched with a real person from our vetted coaching network, chosen for your situation, and they stay with you through the whole fundraising journey.

Matched to your situation

We pair you on two things: what you need to fund, and who has funded it before. Not on who happens to be free that week.

Vetted, not crowdsourced

Every coach comes from our preferred network, screened for real results and real lived experience with the system.

Free, start to finish

Coaching costs you nothing. Not the first call, and not the ones after it.

How it works
